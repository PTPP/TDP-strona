Membership Levels and Development Path

The PCA competency structure sets standards for strictly defined stages of professional advancement.

A

PCA Candidate (Student)

For students and individuals in the preparatory stage. No independent practice under the PCA banner.

Can obtain: access to materials, participation in training, internship/hospitation program.

B

PCA Psychosocial Counsellor – Level 1

Beginner practitioner. Work scope: psychoeducation, support, skills coaching, service orientation, goal-oriented and short-term work.

Profile: documented preparation, verification, mandatory supervision.

C

PCA Psychosocial Counsellor – Level 2

Intermediate practitioner. Structured work, supportive intervention in stress and crises, group work.

Requirements: longer experience, CPD, supervision, audit.

D

PCA Psychosocial Counsellor – Level 3

Advanced practitioner. Complex work, support coordination, interdisciplinary team work, program design.

E

PCA Psychosocial Counsellor – Level 4

Senior practitioner/expert. Contribution to standards, publications, training, environment development.

Additional Endorsements (Optional)

  • PCA Supervisor: From Level 3+ (supervisor program).
  • PCA Trainer / Examiner: From Level 3+.
  • PCA Standards Auditor: From Level 4.

Standards & Central Register

Professional Ethics

Adherence to the PCA code of ethics is the foundation of our practice. Every member is obliged to care for the client’s welfare and the highest standards of confidentiality.

Continuing Professional Development (CPD)

We require our specialists to constantly improve their qualifications. We distinguish active CPD (courses, exams) and passive CPD (publications, reading).

Central Register

Publicly available database of certified specialists, guaranteeing transparency of permissions and qualifications.

Important: All permissions and certificates are publicly verifiable in the Central Register.
